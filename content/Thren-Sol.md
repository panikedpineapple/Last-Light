Thren-Sol, also known as The **Everlight**, is an artificial stellar construct suspended above the city of [[Keth Aurel]]. It is the primary source of light, heat, magical energy and temporal stability within the city's boundaries. It is also the sole reason for the continued survival following the extinction of the true sun.

## Origin 

Thren-sol was forged during the final centuries of the High Arcanum era by an advanced magical civilisation whose remnants now form the foundation of [[Keth aurel]] . The construct is designed to replicate the essential properties of a true star on a local scale, preserving habitability after planetary stellar failure. While numerous artificial stars are believed to have been commisioned, Thren-sol is the only confirmed instance of a completed star.
> [!note]-
> References to 'planatary failure' are theoretical and non-essential. No such event has been confirmed within official records.
> 


## Pyhsical description 

Thren-sol appears as a radiant, sun-like sphere suspended high above the city. Its is made of radiant plasma contained within a shell of layered arcane matrices and glyphs that constantly reconfigure to maintain equilibrium. The containment field anchors it in fixed position relative to the city.

> [!note]-
> Despite malicious rumors, the contanment field remains stable 
> 

## light and colors

Under normal conditions, it emits a warm golden light. In recent decades, observers have documented shifts in hue and intensity, hereby referred as Chromatic Variance Events(CVE).

Documented colors include - 
- gold: baseline, stable magic.
- Purple: brief transitional state; increased emotional sensitivity and prophetic dreams among citizens
- Red: High energy intensity, erratic magical behavior, caution advised.
- Green: only one instance recorded . Unknown effect
> [!note]-
> Emotional instability and Prophetic dream correlations remain anecdotal and unconfirmed.


## Maintenance and Oversight

The Keepers of the light hold exclusive authority over maintenance, calibration and study. Access to the crucible is strictly regulated. Maintenance rituals are conducted continously, combining engineering and spellwork.

> [!note]-
> Use of the term "ritual" is ceremonial, not theological

## Cultural significance

While officially classified as a construct, the everlight is revered by many citizens as a divine presence. The Chorus of Dawn oversee the education material regarding the everlights protection as part of the cohesion initiative.
