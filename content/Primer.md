
No one remembers the sun.
there are stories of course, half-forgotten myths told by scholars and grandma's alike. Tales of a sky filled with light, of gentle warmth and changing seasons. But stories are all they are. The sun is gone, and it has been gone for longer than recorded history can truly agree upon.

There was no dawn when the sun died. No final sunrise, no warning. One day, the sky simply went dark.

The world should have frozen. It should have ended. Instead, it endured; held together by a miracle forged by the brightest minds. The ancients created a Star pf their own; A vast network of arcane engines creating  eternally burning sphere of magic suspended above the world.

Across the darkened world, this miniature sun was a beacon of hope and new beginnings , and around it civilizations bloomed. It's light pushing back the night, warming the land, stabilizing magic and anchoring reality itself. Under it's glow, crops grew, people lived and history continued.

But it was never meant to be permanent, just a stopgap. A miracle built to buy time.

That time is running out.

For decades now, the light has begun to fail. It flickers and dims. It burns strange colors that stain the sky; sickly greens, bruised purples, blood red flares. With each change,reality itself shudders. Magic behaves unpredictably; spells misfire or linger too long. Time stutters, hours stretch,decades pass in the blink of an eye. Something is breaking.

# [[Keth Aurel]]

[[Keth aurel]] is the last bastion of civilization illuminated by civilization artificial star, [[Thren-Sol]]. Roughly organized by the proximity to the warmth and light of the sun. Buildings are curved, faceted and enhanced, so shadows are minimized. 

The city grows organically, streets winding around plazas. terraces and towers of glass and prism directing light. Bright zones closer to the core are open and spacious, rich in warmth and stability, while the over crowed outer districts are dimmer and industrial, hungry for every ray of sunshine. Every corner of the city remains bathed in some measure of light at all time, courtesy of a vast network of mirrors, prisms and magical refractive fields.

# Beyond the light

Beyond the borders of the city, lies a world untouched by the suns light. Adventurers speak of it in hushed tones, describing  a silent cold darkness that seems to stretch forever. Few ever leave the city, and fewer return.

The keepers forbid travel beyond the city's walls except for sanctioned expeditions. Rules have never stopped adventurers though, and the few that return speak of forgotten ruins, vanished towns and strange creatures that move through the dark. There are whispers of long lost magics that lie in wait in half remembered cities; the echoes of a world that once thrived under a real sun.

Even those in the fade, accustomed to dim light, regard the true darkness with dread. It is a place where the light cannot reach, where rules of magics are mere suggestions, a land where the fragile stability of the city does not reach.

# Player section

## What this means for your character

[[Keth aurel]] is not a world of endless horizons or open roads. It is a city clinging to the last light in darkened world. Your characters have lived their entire lives beneath and artificial sun that never sets, in a place where safety and status are shaped by proximity to light.

The campaign will be about survival wrapped in an uneasy comfort, truths buried beneath routine and a world racing towards its demise.there is hope under all the presure, after all the light still shines...... for now.

Soft horror- unease and tension built through routine, atmosphere and discovery rather than over the top gore or brutality. you are surrounded by people yet the world beyond the walls is empty and silent. 

urban fantasy- politics, factions, rumors and relationships matter as much as combat.

#### Things to expect
you will explore a living city shaped by light, interact with powerful factions  and make choices where safety and truth are often at odds. Uncover forgotten knowledge, discover supressed histories and quiet lies, Interspersed with expeditions into places where the everlight does not reach. 


#### Your place in the city

Every person has a relationship with the everlight, whether they want one or not.

Choose or **invent answers** to a few of the following:

- where do you live - Bright inner districts, comfortable middle zone , the dim outer neighborhoods
- How do you feel about the keepers of the light?
- How do you feel about shadows - uncomfortable -> at home 
- What routines keep you grounded? a job, a ritual or a habit, a song or object or just a place you go when  things feel wrong.

Some more ideas to shape the world around your characters. You dont have to answer these exactly, these are meant to be a jumping point into collaboratively creating the world around your characters.

- What does the everlight mean to you personally? Comfort? faith? Control? 
- What part of the city do you avoid and why. a district, a faction or a place tied to a bad encounter or memory
- What rumors do you secretly believe about the city? it doesnt have to be true or something you fully believe in, just something you can't shake.
- Who would notice if you didnt come back home one day? A person, a group or maybe no-one.


> [!important] Please answer these questions.
> - Have you ever ventured into  or seen the night beyond the city's borders? if yes how did it change you
> - Is there something you are afraid will be revealed if the light failed?
> - Do you sing the Bright Song without thinking or do you hesitate?
> - What would you risk the light for?
> -If [[Thren-Sol]] failed tomorrow, who would you try to save first?

Some character archetypes that i think will do well in this campaign -
  - curious types - Lore, secrets, forgotten histories, types who enjoy connecting the dots on fragmented ideas
  - Protective- of children, community, of imperfect systems
  - Skeptics- of Authority, tradition and of "the way it's always been"
  - Explorers: Drawn to the mysteries hiding in plain sight or to the night beyond.
You dont need a complex heroic arc to belong; you just need a reason to care. Please leave your murder hobo boots at the door.


> [!attention] About wizards and magic
> I know i have mentioned chaotic and unpredictable magic a lot, but dont be discouraged from creating wizards. Functionally it will not affect you in the short term. In the long term, i promise the goal is not to nerf wizards or make janky shit just for the sake of *aesthetics*. Fun will always be prioritised and any mechanics that get added in the future  will be debated and implemented only with permission from the players.
