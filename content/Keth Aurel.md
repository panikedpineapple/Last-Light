## The golden city

Keth aurel is widely believed to be the last surviving city of the world, sustained by the artificial star [[Thren-Sol]], also known as the everlight. Built beneath a permanent, manufactured sky, the city is the last bastion in a world where the true sun has long been extinguished. The lands beyond its borders are consumed by the phenomenon known as the True Night.

The city is a self-sufficient megacity; culturally, magically and politically shaped by the proximity to the everlight. Its society, architecture and belief systems are all organized around the management, distribution and preservation of the light.

### History

According to surviving records, the city was founded during the final-era of a high magic civilization, shortly after the extinction of the true sun. Rather than fade quietly into the night, these ancient artificers forged artificial stellar engines; miniature suns to preserve habitable zones.

Most of these artificial stars were either never ignited or failed shortly after. Keth aurel is now regarded as the sole remaining city with an active star.

### Geography

Contrary to popular belief, keth aurel was not designed as a radial city, but as a ever expanding urban mass whose districts are broadly defined by their proximity and access to [[Thren-Sol]]'s light. Elevation, reflector density, architectural design and density determine brightness rather than strict distance alone. As you move further outward from the brightest zones, the light becomes more diffused, filtered and intermittent. Beyond the outer walls, illumination collapses entirely.

The city's infrastructure relies on a impressive system of enchanted mirrors, reflectors prisms and tinted lenses embedded into towers, bridges and rooftops. Primarily used to redirect light into residential and agricultural areas, regulate brightness.

Buildings are designed with curved  facades, stepped profiles and faceted surfaces to minimize shadows. Deep unbroken shadows are culturally frowned upon and in some places, even legally restricted.


### Some prominant areas

**Dawnbelt** - A collective name for the brightest central districts closest to [[Thren-Sol]]. Contains Agricultural terraces, arcane institutions, the headquarters of the keepers of the light and most prominently; the crucible of [[Thren-Sol]]

**Gilded Wilds** - Affluent administrative and mercantile districts. Mostly known for its large noble estates, trade halls and cultural institutions.

**Middle glows** - The most populous regions of the city. home to artisan guild, markets and communal housing. Has regulated light cycles.

**The Fade** - Outer districts with diminished and inconsistent illumination. Patchwork reflectors, informal settlements and communities adapted to low light and scattered shadows.

**Threshold** - Fortified city edge. Large watchtowers with massive arrays of glass and mirror. the expedition launch site.



#### Daily life

Life in the city is structured around light cycles rather than days. There is no true lightless  night within the city limits. Cultural norms place huge importance on brightness etiquette, shadow management and public illumination. Children learn official nursery rhymes praising the everlight. Faith, governance and civic identity are closely intertwined.


### Factions

Magic is directly tied to the everlight. 
- Spellcasting may behave unpredictably during color shifts
- Shadow magic is ~~effective but~~ taboo.
